# The Midnight Gallery — Critical Lock

**Project:** The Case Files  
**Case ID:** Case 001  
**Canon Status:** LOCKED  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Last Updated:** July 19, 2026

## Security-System Interruption

There is no building-wide power outage.

At 10:53 PM, Marcus Reed deliberately interrupts only the museum's security infrastructure:

- Surveillance cameras
- Alarm reporting
- Electronic access monitoring
- Security-event logging
- Selected security-door controls

Building lighting, standard power, climate control, communications, freight/service elevators, and normal building operations remain functional.

The interruption ends at 11:00 PM.

## Culprit

Nina Bell is the sole physical thief. She removes _The Midnight Garden_ during the security interruption, secures the removable inner travel frame inside a modified exhibition-signage case, and directs Jamie Mercer to move it to basement storage.

## Marcus Reed

Marcus caused the interruption as a live test for a future heist controlled by a black-market art broker. He did not expect a theft that night. He later lies that the security network suffered an unexpected system fault.

## Elena Voss

Elena owes $168,000 from her husband's cancer treatment after insurance failed to cover the medical bills. She secretly removed a material sample and helped Nina access provenance records, but did not knowingly assist the theft.

## Theo Crane

Theo recently increased the painting's insured value through a routine pre-exhibition reassessment. He later hides a provenance folder containing a second bill of sale and a Crane family letter acknowledging the suspicious intermediary.

## Nina's Preparation

Nina prepared:

- An adapted release tool
- A modified exhibition-signage case
- Archival foam cut precisely to the inner travel frame
- A duplicate inventory tag taken from another exhibition item
- Knowledge of the service route and freight elevator

## Nina's Lie

Nina admits speaking to Jamie but claims she only repeated Theo's transfer order. She denies preparing or tagging the case and claims she entered the service corridor only after security systems returned online.

## Evidence Structure

The case contains exactly 25 evidence items: five per chapter.

No single clue directly identifies Nina. She is proven through:

1. Advance preparation
2. Technical contact
3. Movement
4. Advance knowledge
5. Concealment and deception

## Enforcement Rules

- Never describe the event as a blackout or power outage.
- The museum remains lit.
- The freight elevator remains operational.
- Chapter 1 item 5 is the gala program preview marked with a time and “E.V.”
- The missing signage case is not introduced in Chapter 1.
- Only two Chapter 5 items directly point toward Nina.
