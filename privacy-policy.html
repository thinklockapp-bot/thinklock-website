Privacy Policy

Summary

ThinkLock is designed to make screen time more productive while minimizing unnecessary data collection. Some data may be stored locally on your device and some limited data may be processed to operate, secure, improve, and support the app. Platform disclosures on the App Store and Google Play should be reviewed alongside this policy.

Information We Collect

• Game progress, scores, streaks, and app preferences
• Performance analytics for adaptive learning and product improvement
• Usage statistics, diagnostics, and crash information
• Account or subscription-related information when required by the app stores or account features

Information We Avoid Collecting Unless Needed

• Camera, microphone, and contact-list access unless clearly required by a future feature
• Financial card details directly inside ThinkLock; purchases are handled by Apple or Google
• Precise location unless a future feature clearly requires it and requests permission
• Data that is not needed to operate, secure, support, or improve the app

Children and Family Use

ThinkLock is designed with safer, more productive screen time in mind. We aim to limit unnecessary data collection from younger users and encourage parents or guardians to review device-level privacy, purchase, and parental-control settings.

Data Storage

• Some information may be stored locally on your device
• Some information may be processed by app platforms, analytics, crash-reporting, or account systems used to operate the app
• You can request account deletion through the app or the Delete Account page
• We use reasonable security practices designed to protect app data

Parental Controls

Parents and guardians can use Apple, Google, and device-level settings to manage downloads, purchases, screen time, and permissions. You can also contact us with privacy questions or account deletion requests.

Contact

Email: privacy@thinklock.app
Subject: "Privacy Policy Question"
Response time: Within 48 hours

ThinkLock is committed to building a safer, more productive brain-training experience. This page should be kept aligned with the live App Store and Google Play data disclosures.
