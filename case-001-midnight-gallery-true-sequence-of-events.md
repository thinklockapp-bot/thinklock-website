# The Midnight Gallery — True Sequence of Events

**Canon Status:** LOCKED  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Last Updated:** July 19, 2026

## Before the Gala

Nina observes part of a rehearsal installation, learns about Marcus's planned security test, and gains archive access through Elena.

At approximately 5:15 PM, Nina photographs the missing bill of sale and sends copies to secure cloud storage, an attorney, and an investigative journalist.

## Gala Night

The gala begins at 7:00 PM.

Nina's modified signage case is staged in a staff corridor. It contains fitted archival foam and a duplicate inventory tag.

Late in the gala, Elena warns Nina not to do anything illegal.

## 10:53 PM — Security Interruption

Marcus manually takes the security systems offline. The museum remains fully lit and operational.

Affected systems include cameras, alarms, access monitoring, security logs, and selected security-door controls.

The freight elevator remains operational.

## Approximately 10:54–10:57 PM

Nina enters the illuminated gallery, releases the mount, removes the inner travel frame, and places it in the modified case.

Elena notices the camera feeds and security indicators are unavailable. She hears movement but assumes security staff are responding.

Theo sees an exhibition case being moved and assumes staff are following his basement-transfer order.

## Approximately 10:58 PM

Nina attaches the duplicate inventory tag and Theo's transfer number.

She instructs Jamie Mercer to move the case down the operational freight elevator to basement storage.

## 11:00 PM

Security systems return online.

## Approximately 11:03 PM

Jamie stores the case in general exhibition storage.

## 11:12 PM and After

The theft is discovered.

Marcus claims the security network failed unexpectedly. Elena conceals her sample. Theo later hides the provenance folder.

Nina claims she entered the service corridor only after security systems returned and denies preparing the specific case.
