Daily Challenge

A fresh challenge every day.

Every day, ThinkLock gives you a fresh 3-game challenge built from randomly selected game types. Play them back-to-back, finish the full series, and build your Brain Score one session at a time.

1

Memorize 4 items

Before the Daily Challenge starts, lock four items into memory.

2

Play 3 random games

Move through three game types in succession until the full series is complete.

3

Recall at the end

After the final game, recall the original items to prove your focus held up.

4

Earn bonus Sparks

Correct recall earns an additional Sparks reward on top of your challenge progress.

Weekly Tournaments

One game takes over the leaderboard.

Each week, one game type becomes the featured tournament. Players compete to post their highest single-session score before the week ends. Basic players ONLY get 1 tournament attempt per day. Pro and Family players can keep chasing their best score with unlimited attempts during the weekly event.

1st

Champion rewards

Win a massive Sparks reward, premium avatar upgrades, and a trophy badge for leaderboard status.

2nd

Finalist flex

Earn a large Sparks reward, recognition, and a badge that shows you finished near the top.

3rd

Top 3 status

Grab bonus Sparks, a Top 3 badge, and extra bragging rights on the leaderboard.

Tournament game types rotate weekly across all 8 games, without repeating the same game back-to-back.
